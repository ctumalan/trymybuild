## Correction 2: Qualify the information-removal promise

In PRODUCT_PLAN.md, the Connected Experience section currently says participants must be free to "remove their information at any time."

This is too absolute because CreatorWorks may eventually need to retain limited information for safety, legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or transaction obligations.

Revise the sentence in plain language so it preserves participant control while acknowledging necessary limits. Use wording equivalent to:

> CreatorWorks should control the continuity and quality of its own experience and the handoff—not control or trap the participant. Also ensure this wording does not imply that CreatorWorks controls or guarantees the quality of an externally hosted application.
