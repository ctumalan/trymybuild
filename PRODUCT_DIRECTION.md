# CreatorWorks product direction

## Decision recorded September 6, 2026: prove value before monetization

The founder prefers the discovery labels **Independent** and **Commercial**.
This is a recorded future direction, not authorization to implement new discovery sections, eligibility restrictions, advertising, billing, or fees now.

CreatorWorks must first demonstrate real value through a working end-to-end experience: genuine creators can publish, visitors can discover and try projects, feedback reaches the right creator, and conversations persist across sessions. Public launch readiness and evidence of useful participation take priority over monetization.

### Intended distinction

- Protect independent creators from being eclipsed by established companies.
- Allow companies to participate transparently and potentially support the platform through clearly identified sponsorship or commercial placements later.
- Preserve human attribution and the connection between a project and its creator.
- Creators receive feedback before facing pressure to sell.

### Open definitions—not finalized eligibility rules

The Independent and Commercial labels are preferred, but their exact eligibility rules remain undecided. In particular, resolve how independently created paid tools and company-created free tools fit before enforcing a classification. Do not silently define Independent as free or Commercial as paid.

Keep creator/entity type, product price, development stage, and sponsorship as separate concepts when designing future data changes. No schema change is approved by this note.

### Monetization gate

Do not activate listing fees, subscriptions, advertising charges, or paid placement based on this discussion. First verify the complete creator and visitor journeys and gather genuine evidence that the platform helps people discover useful projects and gives creators actionable feedback.

The founder will review that evidence before a separate pricing or monetization decision. No launch date, conversion threshold, fee amount, or automatic charging trigger has been established.

### Next priority

Complete the comprehensive connection audit, fix end-to-end blockers, and support the founder's real creator-account and separate visitor-account test. Revisit discovery separation and commercial participation after the website demonstrates value.
