# Homepage clarity and light theme

The homepage now leads with “Share what you are building. Find apps worth trying.” beside the logo. Share your app and Find an app select separate URL and search modes. Sharing starts with Continue and explains the private-sharing/public-catalog choice; submitting a URL saves a draft rather than publishing.

The listing journey retains the homepage frame and persistent browsing control. Switching to browsing and back keeps the current question and draft. Authentication still uses the existing hosted sign-in provider.

The warm light theme and violet actions apply across platform surfaces. App details use a quieter creator byline, serif headline and integrated guidance. Catalog Save and Share controls remain visible without hover. Submit a wish opens an accessible dialog, preserves the draft, and resumes after sign-in instead of rendering a permanent bottom form.

## Validation

- 242 automated tests pass, including listing/search separation, draft preservation, all listing-stage navigation, wish categories and submission safeguards.
- Type checking and production build pass; no database migration is required.
- Local browser checks cover sharing/search modes, same-page URL submission, browsing and resuming a draft, persistent card actions, and wish-dialog opening and Escape dismissal.
- No real accounts, listings, wishes, feedback or invitations were submitted during verification.
- Generated poster proposals and unused example portraits are excluded from this release.
